Storm briefing

Main-chain Bitcoin settles like a heavy court ledger: deliberate, global, expensive if you use it for every coffee. Lightning is the side street where tabs stay open between people who transact often, then settle back when it matters.

You do not need to run a node to understand the metaphor. You need to feel the tradeoff: speed and low fees versus channel setup, liquidity, and new failure modes.
