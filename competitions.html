LayerQuest: Colaso Night Watch

A timed educational hunt across the Zoo. Follow weekly clues, visit habitats, learn Bitcoin layers. Finish before the season ends.

1 August 2026 → 12 September 2026 · 6 weeks

How to play

  1. Read this week’s clue below (no spoilers for locked weeks).
  2. Visit the habitat it points to: Colaso, Bitcoin Base Camp, or Lightning Storm Run.
  3. Do the learning challenge (written reflection or classroom activity). Educators: run it as a short session.
  4. Log your seal in Telegram t.me/LayerZoo with week number + one sentence you learned.

This is a learning quest, not a prize raffle. Recognition goes to clear thinking and consistency, not speed-running.
