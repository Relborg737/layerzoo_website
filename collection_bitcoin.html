Campfire story

Base Camp is where explorers stop pretending Bitcoin is only a price ticker. Here the questions are slower: What is scarce? Who holds the keys? What does “don’t trust, verify” feel like in ordinary language?

Full official art and NFT series for this habitat will land later. Soft-open means the education enclosure is open now, so LayerQuest and classrooms are not staring at a grey “coming soon” box.

Challenge: “Explain it to a sceptical friend”

In under 120 words, explain Bitcoin to someone smart who thinks it’s only gambling. You must include:

  • one idea about limited supply
  • one idea about who controls the money
  • one honest risk or tradeoff (no hype)

Post your paragraph in t.me/LayerZoo with tag BaseCamp, or keep it private in a journal if you’re teaching offline.
